# Afterimage prompt set

## Primary membrane material

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: reusable full-bleed website hero photograph
Primary request: A dark cinematic macro photograph of one thin translucent off-white woven membrane suspended in velvet-black space. It forms one long continuous ridge and one soft fold; micro-fibers, edge fraying, weave density, translucency and thickness are visible.
Composition: horizontal 16:9; material enters from lower-right and arcs toward center-left, leaving a large dark safe area at upper-left and along the top.
Lighting: one narrow cold-white grazing beam from upper-right with subtle bounce and detailed shadows.
Style: photorealistic contemporary exhibition material study, not abstract CGI.
Avoid: text, UI, logos, watermark, glowing orbs, neon edges, blue-purple gradients, fog, stars, cyberpunk or CGI.
```

## Secondary specimen material

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: reusable secondary archive image
Primary request: A precise macro photograph of a thin circular smoked-glass disc containing an irregular branching silver-grey mineral or conductive-ink trace. A single deep-red registration thread crosses the edge once.
Composition: disc in the lower-left third at a slight oblique angle; large untouched black field on the right and top.
Lighting: narrow soft white edge light and faint overhead reflection showing glass thickness.
Style: scientific object study and contemporary archive photography, restrained and mysterious.
Avoid: text, labels, laboratory props, people, logos, watermark, glowing energy, galaxies, neon, sci-fi HUD, fantasy or CGI.
```

## Archive page

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: desktop Archive page for a cinematic personal website
Primary request: A shippable 16:9 velvet-black retrieval page. A narrow left rail contains identity, search, type filters and vertical progress. The middle contains six widely spaced title lines with sequence numbers and tiny image slits, without boxes. Active row 01 AFTERIMAGE is cold white with one deep-red cursor. The right field reveals an enormous very dark membrane crop; a small circular specimen crop appears as the next-object trace.
Style: cinematic archive and contemporary exhibition interface, never sci-fi HUD. Narrow grotesk, small serif annotation, subtle grain.
Interaction: active image crossfade and cursor travel.
Avoid: cards, rounded rectangles, dashboards, film-reel clichés, neon, blue-purple gradients, glassmorphism and chronology.
```

## Detail page

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: desktop content-detail page
Primary request: A shippable 16:9 detail opening for AFTERIMAGE. Velvet-black canvas; the membrane fills the upper-right and fades into black at left. A slim left rail holds identity, back navigation and reading progress. The title spans the lower-left; a readable serif abstract and compact metadata sit to its right; the circular specimen is cropped at the lower-right as NEXT OBJECT. A single deep-red hairline aligns title, progress and next-object cue.
Metadata: FORM PROJECT; MEDIUM MIXED; STATUS ONGOING.
Style: contemporary art monograph and exhibition film title sequence, precise and readable.
Interaction: slow image drift, underlined READ NOTES, next-object reveal.
Avoid: cards, dashboard UI, neon, blue-purple gradients, cyberpunk HUD, fake affiliations, timelines and WebGL-dependent effects.
```
