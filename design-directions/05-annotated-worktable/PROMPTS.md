# Prompt set

## 01 Material and component board

```text
Create a 16:9 implementation-oriented material-system board on a warm-grey tabletop viewed almost top-down. Use one upper-left light source. Show five components: an off-white cotton paper card with restrained letterpress, a charcoal-black fiber card with blind emboss, an authentic instant-film photograph of a neutral workbench, a small stitched textile swatch, and a thin oxidized metal tag. Keep one clear primary component and generous spacing. Add exactly three short digital annotation lines, each anchored to a visible component edge. All floating labels use one neutral neo-grotesk family, one weight and two sizes. Preserve paper fibers, instant-film chemistry, stitching, scratches and oxidation. Avoid scrapbook clutter, mixed fonts, disconnected lines, giant type, cards-as-UI, tape, logos and watermark.
```

## 02 Light homepage

```text
Create a shippable 16:9 light personal homepage called Annotated Worktable. Use a warm mineral-grey paper/plaster field, almost top-down camera and one soft upper-left light. Arrange exactly five physical objects: one primary instant-film workbench photograph overlapping off-white and charcoal paper cards, one narrow technical strip, one small stitched textile swatch and one oxidized metal tag. Keep the textile under eight percent of the viewport. Print TY H. onto the substrate and let the central stack obscure part of it. Use one neo-grotesk family for every floating interface label. Add three short, physically anchored annotations: 01 / PHOTO, 02 / STITCH and 03 / METAL. Use no invented content titles, no giant typography, no disconnected lines, no mixed digital fonts and no card grid.
```

## 03 Dark homepage

```text
Create the dark-theme counterpart of the same Annotated Worktable homepage. Preserve the exact object constellation and hierarchy. Replace the pale substrate with tactile charcoal-black fiber paper. Keep the instant-film border and cotton paper warm; reveal the black card with embossed edges and low-reflective highlights; give the metal one narrow rim light. Blind-emboss TY H. into the substrate. Keep the same single neo-grotesk interface and three anchored labels. Do not turn the page into a cinematic poster, add neon, introduce a giant title or change the composition.
```

## 04 Photo detail

```text
Create the 16:9 detail state reached by clicking the homepage instant photograph. Enlarge the same physical photograph to roughly half the viewport while retaining its border and contact shadow. Move the paper caption surface to the right; keep the black card behind the photo; recede textile and metal to the edges. Print WORKING NOTE / 01 on the substrate behind the photo so it is partially obscured. Use exactly two semantic annotations anchored to visible details inside the image: MEASURE at the ruler and SURFACE at the copper sheet. Keep one digital font family and continuous object identity so GSAP Flip can reproduce the transition. Avoid a modal frame, mixed fonts, disconnected lines, oversized title and fake biography.
```
