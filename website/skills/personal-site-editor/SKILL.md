# Personal site editor

The active site is generated by `website/build.mjs` into `material-demo/dist/site/`. Edit source files in `website/`; do not edit generated HTML. Read [architecture.md](../../architecture.md) for source ownership, shared layout/type controls, language workflow, and the record component. Read [site-map.md](references/site-map.md) for routes and the map's factual boundaries. For Notes writing or a new article, also read [notes.md](references/notes.md).

Preserve the owner's factual boundaries. Do not invent work results, images of their work, personal stories, addresses, travel, publications, or roles. An editorial illustration can identify a record but must not be presented as a research figure or project screenshot. Keep team awards identified as team awards. Keep original PDF files unmodified and preview them with the local PDF.js viewer.

Current direction: compact clear typography, generous but purposeful space, a selective Home, separate Work and Notes, About with one canonical experience record set reached through cities and institutions, ordinary scrolling, contact options opening in place, and a restrained persistent music corner. Images should adapt to light and dark backgrounds. These are design decisions to assess against new user feedback, not a fixed template.

For layout corrections, change the shared `site.css` tokens and the owning component rule. Avoid adding another late override for a margin, font size, or page edge that already has a shared control. For substantive About copy, edit paired `zh`/`en` values in `about-content.mjs`; `check.mjs` checks completeness. The Traditional Chinese page is generated from Chinese. Merge learning and vulnerable personal prose in the real About note. Give the interactive software-style workbench its own section and give music an independent section. Read [music.md](references/music.md) when adding songs, albums or listening notes. Retain overlapping real sleeves on a pale line, spreading on hover/focus, in one compact, horizontally scrollable strip generated from data. The standalone network and discipline labels were removed after the owner’s correction; do not restore them as a feature. Keep the personal prose conversational and open-ended, without an identity slogan or an imposed uplifting conclusion. The owner’s “学得很杂” is conversational background, not preferred website wording: describe different education, cultures and ways of thinking, including their conflicts. Preserve the gap between how the owner wants to act and how they sometimes act; do not sanitize mistakes into strengths or turn contradictions into personality labels. Named emotions are examples, not categories; do not bring back emotion cards or photo assignments. Preserve the tentative “怪人” thought as uncertainty. Study, experience, honours, credentials and reviewing remain separate factual records. Use the six shared type roles at a 13px root (15px A+); see architecture.md for ownership and exceptions. For personal illustrations, use genuinely transparent isolated objects and tiny loose doodles beside text. Avoid detailed complete watercolor scenes, rooms, emotional categories or soft lifestyle imagery. Do not write appeals to the reader to appreciate the owner’s sincerity, care or effort: demonstrate those through content and craft. Read `website/references/about-illustrations.md` for generated asset provenance and prompts. Use existing margins rather than adding a gallery or more card rows. Drawings are editorial metaphors, not personal documentary evidence. Treat this as a working design, subject to the owner's visual feedback.

When editing, build with `node website/build.mjs`, check with `node website/check.mjs`, and inspect affected routes in the browser at desktop and narrow widths. The tw edition is generated by opencc-js conversion of the zh render — write source copy in simplified Chinese and let the build convert; `check.mjs` fails if simplified characters, wrong `html lang` codes, or missing icon files reach the output, so treat those failures as regressions in the generation pipeline. State which parts were actually verified. Ask the owner only for missing personal facts or a design choice that materially changes the result; make other reversible improvements directly.
