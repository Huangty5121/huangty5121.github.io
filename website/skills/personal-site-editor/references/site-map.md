# Active website map

- `website/views.mjs`: page structures for three language editions and the relation between Home, Work, Notes, About, practice pages, writing, and PDF reading. About's personal section renders copy from `website/about-content.mjs`.
- `website/about-content.mjs`: paired Chinese/English About copy, discipline names and personal reflection, and the expandable real album list and workbench panel copy. See `website/architecture.md` before changing language, typography, or shared alignment.
- `website/content.mjs`: website-specific copy over the historical records in `material-demo/dist/desk-data.mjs`.
- `website/entries.mjs`: Work index metadata, order, tags, and cover filenames. The sole current Notes draft is in `website/industrial-note.mjs`.
- `website/cover-art.mjs`: abstract SVG covers. Keep their canvas transparent and check both themes.
- `website/site.css` and `website/site.mjs`: shared page-width, gutter and text-size variables; responsive layout; navigation, city selection, theme, the About network, music, and disclosure interactions.
- `website/build.mjs`: generates zh-Hans, en, and tw routes; the tw edition is the zh render converted with opencc-js (`cn`→`hk`), and `tw/site.mjs` is a converted copy whose module-relative asset imports are rewritten to `../assets/`. It also prunes stale asset files from the committed dist tree and fingerprints `site.css`/`site.mjs`/the hero photo into a `?v=` cache key. `website/check.mjs` checks links, PDF identity, `html lang` codes, empty titles, unconverted simplified characters in tw output, icon file presence, and exact-case link resolution (deploy hosts are case-sensitive).
- `website/map-geometry.mjs` and `website/tools/derive-map.py`: city-centre coordinates used by the About locator's city framing. Data provenance is in [`geography.md`](../../../references/geography.md). The visible basemap uses OpenStreetMap raster tiles drawn by Leaflet; WGS-84 coordinates are used directly in `site.mjs`. Organisation pin positions in `views.mjs` are owner-verified.
- `website/assets/harbour-night.jpg`: the Home hero photograph (Unsplash, pourya gohari), versioned by the build cache key. The cutout material image also appears beneath the Home notes teaser.

Main routes: `index.html`, `collection.html`, `notes.html`, `about.html`, `news.html`; details: `writing-modernization.html`, two practice pages, publication records, and three original-PDF readers. The retired `writing.html` route redirects to About's short reflection. zh-Hans routes are at `/site/`; English at `/site/en/`; Traditional Chinese at `/site/tw/`. The header language switcher (简/繁/EN) is refreshed on soft navigation. Contact is a local popover, not a page jump. The audio player persists across same-language navigation and plays an official short preview only.

Paper reading pages use a viewport-height original-PDF preview with a compact contextual sidebar on desktop; the preview precedes the sidebar on mobile. PDF.js starts at page-fit and retains search, paging, and zoom. The colour system in `site.css` uses teal for interactive paths, green for geographic surfaces, and warm clay for notes/material accents; check both themes and reduced-motion behaviour before changing it.

About uses four navigation chapters: a continuous personal note without emotion categories, an interactive workbench, an independent music corner, and the factual records. The factual records follow under Study, experience & honours. The map opens on a world overview with Beijing and Greater Bay Area region pins. A region opens its detailed map, then city and institution controls show the same shared experience records. The map panel names only the region until a pin is clicked; after a click it names that institution and its recorded roles. The map groups institutions by *institution city*, not the owner's travel history or exact work site. Current pin groups: Beijing (Tsinghua; IGSNRR at 11A Datun Road; Qiyuan in the Haidian Wenquan environmental park; Iluvatar at 36 Haidian West Street), Shenzhen (SMART, X-Institute with Pingshan and Nanshan sites), Hong Kong (PolyU with its Hung Hom campus plus the Hung Hom Bay and West Kowloon CPCE/HKCC campuses — CPCE has no institution button of its own, its study and records open under PolyU; Royal Plaza at Mong Kok East; HKSAR Government at the Tamar Central Government Offices plus a Yau Tsim Mong site at the District Office building that opens the same government records). The PolySmart appointment is recorded under PolyU and has no separate pin; institution addresses were checked against [Tsinghua's campus handbook](https://is.tsinghua.edu.cn/Campus-Life-Handbook.pdf), [IGSNRR's contact page](https://english.igsnrr.cas.cn/about/contact/), and OpenStreetMap/Nominatim lookups on 2026-09-25 (Royal Plaza, Mong Kok Government Offices, both CPCE campuses, Central Government Offices, the three corrected Beijing sites). New groupings require an explicit source or owner confirmation. Changing the map must preserve the single shared experience record set.

The current design is intentionally editable. New images or widgets should clarify a record or action. Check desktop and 320–430px effective widths in all three language editions, especially the header, expanding records, map, popovers, and PDF reader. Prefer semantic native controls and reduced-motion support. Update `website/README.md` and `website/design-qa.md` when the architecture or verified state changes.
