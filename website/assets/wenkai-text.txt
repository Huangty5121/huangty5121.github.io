Yet form rarely arrives only at the end: the way a material bends, how parts join, and whether someone wants to pick an object up all pull it in a direction.form01造型不只是外壳Form is more than a shell我们谈过一个很小、却很容易被忽略的问题：简约是不是一定更好？如果删去一个构件，制造更容易了，却让产品失去辨识度或使用时的线索，这种“简化”未必真的改善了它。外观也有实际后果。它会影响人怎样理解产品、愿不愿意接近它，以及在许多选择之间为什么停在这一件前面。这不等于让好看凌驾于功能之上，而是承认功能、感受与市场判断本来就会相互牵动。A small question from our design discussion is whether simpler is always better. Removing a component may make manufacturing easier, yet also erase a cue that helps people understand the product or recognize it among alternatives.Appearance has consequences. It affects how a product is read, whether someone approaches it, and why a person might choose it. This does not put beauty above function; it recognizes that function, experience, and a decision to buy can influence one another.modernity02现代化不是一种固定风格Modernization is not a single style如果把“中国现代化”直接译成某一种流行外观，就会跳过更重要的设计问题：谁在使用，怎样生产，维修和更新由谁承担，新的生活方式又需要什么样的物件？同一种材料语言，放在不同的供应、成本与使用条件下，可能作出完全不同的选择。因此，讨论工业设计与现代化的关系，不能只把一组造型当成时代的答案；还要看支撑那种造型的系统与日常生活。If “Chinese modernization” is reduced to a fashionable visual style, harder design questions disappear: who uses the object, how is it made, who maintains it, and what does a changing way of life actually require?The same material language may lead to different choices under different production, cost, and use conditions. A visual vocabulary alone cannot answer what modernization means for industrial design; the systems and everyday lives around it matter too.decision03让选择有依据Making a choice defensible一个构件可以因为结构、触感、辨识度或情绪价值而留下；也可以因为它制造复杂、难以维护，或者与真正的使用情境无关而被拿掉。设计不需要预先站在“多”或“少”的一边。更值得追问的是：这个形式回应了什么条件？如果换一种方案，谁会得到好处，又是谁承担代价？把这些关系说明白，造型才不只是审美宣言，而成为可以讨论的设计判断。A component may remain because of structure, touch, recognition, or emotional value. It may go because it complicates production, resists repair, or adds little to the actual situation of use. Design does not need to choose “more” or “less” in advance.The useful question is what conditions a form responds to. Under an alternative, who benefits and who bears the cost?
