简约就一定好吗Is simpler always better?简约不代表就是好的设计。每个 component 要有意义，但这个意义不一定只是功能。比如外观。它可能没让一个东西更耐用，也没让生产更方便，但它让人愿意买，那也不能说它没用。所以还是得看具体在做什么，有没有其他方案，选哪个更合适。不能因为说了“做减法”，就默认减完会更好。Simplicity does not automatically make a design good. Each component should have a reason to exist, but that reason need not be purely functional.Appearance may not improve durability or manufacturing, yet it can matter to someone deciding what to buy.The choice still depends on the situation and the alternatives.
